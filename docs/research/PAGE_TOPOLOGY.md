# ElevateLP Page Topology

Target URL: `https://landingpages.elevatelp.com.br/`

## Global Layout
- Single-page Elementor landing page on a black background.
- Desktop content is centered in a `1140px` max-width column with `90px` top/bottom padding.
- Mobile content is a single centered column with tighter side padding and full-width form card.
- No header/navigation. No sticky elements.

## Sections
1. **Hero Header**
- Contains small VA icon, arc background image, eyebrow, headline, and subtitle.
- Static with entrance animation classes on the original; no scroll-dependent behavior observed.

2. **Question Form Card**
- Dark radial-gradient card with 25px desktop radius and dark border.
- Two radio choices with custom dark rounded labels.
- Pink gradient WhatsApp CTA with large glow and shine highlight.
- Interaction model: click-driven radio selection only. Original form posts through Elementor and does not expose a direct WhatsApp URL in the static HTML.

3. **Footer**
- Pink radial separator line.
- Logo image plus two-line copyright text.
- Second separator line below.
